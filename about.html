About this site

This is a free, no-frills practice quiz site for AP US Government students. Every week, a new short quiz goes up covering whatever our class covered that week -- so you can quickly check what stuck and what needs another look.

It's built and maintained by a student, for students. There's no login, no account, no tracking, and nothing is saved anywhere except inside your own browser while you're taking a quiz -- close the tab and it's gone.

How it works

Each quiz shows one multiple-choice question at a time. Questions and answer choices are shuffled every time you load a quiz, so retaking one isn't just memorizing option positions. When you pick an answer, you'll immediately see whether it was correct along with a short explanation. At the end, you'll get your score and a list of exactly which questions you missed.

Disclaimer: Some quiz content on this site is AI-assisted, and AI-generated material can contain mistakes. Treat these quizzes as a helpful study aid to supplement your own review -- not a substitute for your textbook, class notes, or teacher. If something here looks wrong, don't take it as gospel.
