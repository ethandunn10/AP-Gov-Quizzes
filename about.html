About Knotee

Knotee is a free site of practice questions for AP® classes. It was built by a high schooler who got tired of photographing textbook pages, uploading them to ChatGPT, and asking it to make a quiz -- over and over, every unit. This is that, without the re-uploading.

Why "Knotee"

Tying a knot in a string is the oldest memory trick there is: you make something hard to forget by giving it a place to hold on to. That is what answering a question does. Pulling information out of your head is harder -- and sticks better -- than reading it again, and every question you answer ties another knot. Knotee is a page full of them.

What's covered

Knotee is built to cover every AP® subject. Live right now are AP® U.S. Government and Politics and AP® Biology, each with about 600 questions organized by unit, so you can drill one unit at a time instead of wading through everything at once. More subjects are on the way.

What makes it different

Mastery is tracked per unit, not as one overall score -- a 78% average hides the fact that you have Unit 3 cold and Unit 5 not at all, and Knotee shows you that split. Questions you miss also come back later instead of disappearing once you've seen them, so a wrong answer turns into another rep rather than a dead end.

How it works

Each quiz shows one multiple-choice question at a time. Questions and answer choices are shuffled every time you load a quiz, so retaking one isn't just memorizing option positions. When you pick an answer, you'll immediately see whether it was correct along with a short explanation. At the end, you'll get your score and a list of exactly which questions you missed.

There's no login and no account. Your progress is saved in your own browser, not on a server.

Heads up: Knotee's questions are AI-generated, and AI-generated material can contain errors. Spot-check anything you plan to rely on, and treat these questions as a supplement to your textbook, class notes, and teacher -- not a replacement for them.
